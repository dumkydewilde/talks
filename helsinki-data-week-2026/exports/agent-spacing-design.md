# Measured Traces

A warm editorial field gives technical evidence room to breathe. Large, direct titles establish the subject, while a disciplined grid keeps dense observations orderly. Space separates claims from measurements rather than disguising uncertainty. Every alignment should feel meticulously crafted.

Charcoal supplies structure; sky blue identifies agent-to-agent activity; garden green distinguishes communication with a person. Color is a consistent information system across both images, not decoration. The restrained MotherDuck palette and Aeonik Fono / Aeonik Mono maintain continuity with the existing social image.

Repeated traces and unedited text are the central material. The conceptual thread is a change in communication texture, not a claim about invented language, intent, token savings, or correctness. Per-writer paths must remain separate. The image prioritizes the observation; methodological context lives in these notes rather than competing with the graphic.

One square presents the visualization with its metric, legend, sample context, and reported summaries. Its companion presents all three curated excerpts exactly as written, with wrapping introduced only by layout. Typography differentiates observation from quotation without changing the spelling, punctuation, capitalization, or spaces of the source. This demands painstaking attention and master-level execution.

Both compositions should show the product of deep expertise through crisp rendering, consistent margins, and carefully checked text boundaries. Remove presentation controls, event furniture, and private links; retain a quiet MotherDuck logo. Export two 1200 × 1200 PNGs without changing the deck, and refine through spacing and scale rather than added ornament.
