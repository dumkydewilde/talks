### Adjust your process
# It's not a demo. It's a recipe.
There's no one-time fix: prompts drift, comments go stale. You improve it on purpose, and you measure.
- Write an eval set: 25 real questions + gold answers
- Test your baseline: accuracy, turns, cost
- Test every change against held-out questions
- Capture telemetry, promote real misses back into evals
