### Why any of this matters on a Saturday
# Your job is the context, not the code.
</Top>

<Block icon="fa:brain" title="01 · Let it fetch its own context" color="sky">Stop pasting. Point it at the file, the folder, the URL, the error message, and let the loop go and read it. Every tool call is context you did not have to think about.</Block>
<Block icon="fa:list-check" title="02 · Context is a budget" color="sun">Everything it has read stays in the room, including the dead ends and the three fixes that failed. A long muddy chat gives worse answers than a short clean one.</Block>
<Block icon="fa:check" title="03 · So: small steps, fresh starts" color="garden">One checkable thing per trip round the loop. When it starts drifting, open a new chat and give it the two sentences that matter. That is the entire craft.</Block>
