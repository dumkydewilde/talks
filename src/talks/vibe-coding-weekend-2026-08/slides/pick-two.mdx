### The only law of weekend projects
# Pick two. Honestly.
Every project you start today sits somewhere in this triangle, and you do not get all three corners.

The trick is not working faster. It is shrinking the ambition until finishing it fits inside one free Sunday, then letting the thing grow next weekend if it earned it.

<Callout box accent="sun" label="Reminder" title="A finished small thing beats an abandoned big one.">The kids' games are seven evenings, not one project.</Callout>
